The website feels like one brand, but its content and navigation are not equally finished everywhere. The shared dark palette, Geist typography, purple actions, photography, header, and footer are a solid foundation. The biggest opportunity is to make every destination feel equally ready and every repeated interaction predictable.

Design specificity is moderately strong. Taipei/San Francisco imagery, real founders, recognizable companies, and concrete program information make this recognizably 886 Studios. The violet glows and gradients are conventional; the real people and community are the distinctive part. Preserve those.

Three strengths deserve preservation:

- Shared branding already connects the main page families well.
- Portfolio rows, editorial news layouts, portraits, and program photography fit their different content.
- Contact clearly separates founder and general inquiries; the 404 provides a straightforward recovery action.
